Privacy Policy

Local YouTube Transcript & Summary · Last updated: 2026

This extension and its optional local helper run entirely on your own computer. This policy explains, in plain language, what does and does not happen to your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage statistics. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no account, and no advertising.

What the extension does with data on your device

  • It reads the URL of the YouTube watch page currently open in your browser, so it can ask the local helper for that video’s caption track.
  • It displays the returned captions and a locally computed summary inside the page.
  • All of this happens in memory in your browser and is discarded when the tab closes.

Where data goes

The video URL is sent only to 127.0.0.1 / localhost — a helper program that you install and run yourself on the same computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension makes no other network requests and bundles all of its own code (no remote code is downloaded or executed).

The local helper

The optional helper uses the open-source tool yt-dlp to retrieve the caption track of the requested video from YouTube. Its behavior is governed by that tool and by YouTube’s own terms; the helper runs under your control on your machine.

Data retention

None, because no data is collected or stored by the developer.

Children’s privacy

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
